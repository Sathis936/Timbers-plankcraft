Our Origin & Philosophy

We Don't Lay Floors. We Cultivate Generational Foundations.

In 1994, master woodwright Alistair Vance established Timber & Plank as an antidote to fast-milled, chemically saturated synthetic laminate flooring. Disenchanted by drum-sander gouges and toxic formaldehyde glues flooding the building industry, Vance united a collective of Bavarian cabinetmakers, French parquet restorers, and Japanese temple joiners under one studio roof.

Every timber plank that leaves our atelier undergoes an 18-month natural equilibrium seasoning process before it touches a saw blade. We examine growth rings to understand the tree's sun exposure, grain density, and internal fiber tension.

When an architect or homeowner commissions Timber & Plank, they receive an enduring heirloom that flexes harmoniously with seasons, breathes cleanly without VOC off-gassing, and grows richer with natural age and family footfalls.

The Guild Standards

Four Pillars of Architectural Timber Craft

Every project is governed by strict technical codes certified by the National Wood Flooring Association and European Timber Guild.

01
PEFC & FSC

Sustainable Old-Growth Forests

We select single trees from certified European and North American managed forests, never clear-cutting. Three indigenous saplings are planted for every mature timber harvested.

Ethical Forestry Provenance
02
Slow Cured

18-Month Air Seasoning

Rapid artificial kiln heating shocks wood cells and creates internal fractures. We air-season all lumber slowly in microclimate sheds until moisture stabilizes at a perfect 7–9%.

Zero Warping Guarantee
03
Hand-Planed

Precision Tongue & Groove

Our mill cutters carve tolerances down to 0.1mm. Pillowed micro-bevels are hand-scraped with Japanese water stones to create smooth walking barefoot luxury underfoot.

0.1mm Mechanical Precision
04
Laser Guided

Subfloor Science & Acoustics

A floor is only as durable as its foundation. We laser-level concrete slabs to within 1.5mm, test in-situ relative humidity with ASTM digital probes, and decouple acoustic footsteps.

STC & IIC 72+ Sound Rating
