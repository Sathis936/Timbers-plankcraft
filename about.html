Our Origin & Philosophy

We Don't Lay Floors. We Cultivate Generational Foundations.

In 1994, master woodwright Alistair Vance established Timber & Plank as an antidote to fast-milled, chemically saturated synthetic laminate flooring. Disenchanted by drum-sander gouges and toxic formaldehyde glues flooding the building industry, cabinetmakers, French and Japanese temple joiners under one studio roof.

Every timber plank that leaves our atelier undergoes an 18-month natural equilibrium seasoning process before it touches a saw blade. We examine growth rings to understand the tree's sun exposure, grain density, and internal fiber tension.

When an architect or homeowner commissions Timber & Plank, they receive an enduring heirloom that flexes harmoniously with seasons, breathes cleanly without VOC off-gassing, and grows richer with natural age and family footfalls.

The Guild Standards

Four Pillars of Architectural Timber Craft

Every project is governed by strict technical codes certified by the National Wood Flooring Association and European Timber Guild.

01
PEFC & FSC

Sustainable Old-Growth Forests

We select single trees from certified European and North American managed forests, never clear-cutting. Three indigenous saplings are planted for every mature timber harvested.

Ethical Forestry Provenance
02
Slow Cured

18-Month Air Seasoning and Planed

Rapid artificial kiln heating shocks wood cells and creates internal fractures. We air-season all lumber slowly in microclimate sheds until moisture stabilizes at a perfect 7–9%.

Zero Warping Guarantee
03
Hand-Planed

Precision Tongue & Groove

Our mill cutters carve tolerances down to 0.1mm. Pillowed micro-bevels are hand-scraped with Japanese water stones to create smooth walking barefoot luxury underfoot.

0.1mm Mechanical Precision
04
Laser Guided

Subfloor Science & Acoustics

A floor is only as durable as its foundation. We laser-level concrete slabs to within 1.5mm,relative humidity with ASTM digital probes, and decouple acoustic footsteps.

STC & IIC 72+ Sound Rating

The World's Best certified Hardwood Reserves

✦
European White Oak (Quercus Robur) — Janka 1,360 lbf: Grown in chalky soils of Tronçais & Spessart. Delivers cathedral heartwood grain and high natural tannin density suited for reactive smoking and matte hardwax oils.
✦
American Black Walnut (Juglans Nigra) — Janka 1,010 lbf: Sustainably harvested in Appalachian valleys. Steam-treated to infuse rich violet and dark espresso hues completely through the sapwood for velvety barefoot softness.
✦
Brazilian Ipe & Cumaru — Janka 3,680 lbf: Amazonian ironwood density naturally impervious to marine saltwater, rot, and termites with a Class-A fire rating identical to concrete.
✦
Canadian Sugar Maple & Teak — Janka 1,450 / 1,150 lbf: Ultra-tight closed grain Canadian hard maple for high-traffic commercial spaces, alongside Burmese Royal Teak featuring natural silicon oil for luxury yacht decks.
