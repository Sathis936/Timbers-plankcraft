Site Measurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the site measurement genuinely free?

Yes. Our laser site measurement and subfloor moisture evaluation are completely free with zero obligation. You will receive an exact itemized quote in Rupees (₹) valid for 60 days.

Do you bring real wood samples to our home?

Absolutely. Wood changes dramatically depending on natural sunlight exposure and artificial lighting temperature. Our master estimator travels with a portable trunk containing large-format planks and finishes.

How long does the in-home measurement visit take?

Typically between 30 to 45 minutes for standard residences up to 2,000 square feet. This allows sufficient time to test subfloor levelness, measure doors and transitions, and discuss finish options.
