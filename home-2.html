The 4-Stage Architectural Protocol

Timber moves with climate and seasons. Our protocol guarantees mathematical permanence and flawless tactile beauty.

01

Moisture & Laser Scan

In-situ RH probe testing and 3D subfloor topography scan before timber acclimation.

02

Acoustic Decoupling

Elastomeric polyurethane adhesive membrane prevents hollow reverberation and structural shear.

03

Micro-Bevel Joinery

04

Zero-VOC Bio-Oil Curing

Hand-buffed vegetable oil finishes penetrate deep into wood fibers, curing to satin perfection.
