Studio Compliance & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026 • Timber & Plank Craft Studio & Architectural Showroom

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our showroom, request free timber swatch sample kits, or schedule an in-home laser site measurement, we collect personal information necessary to fulfill your architectural wood flooring consultation. This includes your name, verified telephone number, email address, physical installation site address, and specific hardwood species preferences.

2. Architectural Confidentiality & Blueprint Data

Architectural blueprints, CAD drawings, site measurement laser telemetry, and private residential addresses shared with Timber & Plank Craft Studio are treated with strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your proprietary blueprints or personal contact information with third-party advertising vendors.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To dispatch sample swatch boxes directly to your residence via insured courier.
  • To calibrate precision laser site measurement schedules with senior field evaluators.
  • To generate customized square-foot material estimates and subfloor moisture diagnostics.
  • To transmit quarterly editions of The Timber Journal (upon explicit voluntary subscription).

4. Cookies & Local Storage Preferences

Our website uses minimal local storage solely to retain your chosen theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode), layout direction (LTR or RTL), and the active contents of your Free Sample Swatch Box. These cookies contain no personally identifiable marketing tracking pixels.

5. Data Deletion & Concierge Contact

You retain the full legal right to inspect, correct, or request the immediate deletion of your contact records and site measurement history from our studio register. Please email concierge@timberplankcraft.com with your request.
