Engineered for Rain, Sun & Barefoot Comfort

Standard outdoor decks rot from the substructure upwards. Timber & Plank exterior systems incorporate marine flashing, joist protective membrane tape, and gravity slope drainage.

01
Joist Membrane

Substructure Weather Shielding

We apply self-sealing butyl joist barrier tape over all pressure-treated substructure framing. This stops water pooling on screw entry points and triples the life of deck joists.

  • ✓ 100% butyl polymer self-sealing tape
  • ✓ Hot-dipped galvanized & marine stainless hardware
  • ✓ Sub-deck laser slope alignment prevents standing puddles
Prevents Sub-Frame Rot
02
316 Stainless

Concealed Stainless Clip Fastening

Never step on hot screw heads or splintered counter-bores. Hardwood boards are grooved along side edges to accept 316-grade marine stainless steel clips that regulate expansion gaps automatically.

  • ✓ 316-Marine stainless steel rust-free guarantee
  • ✓ Uniform 4mm ventilation & drainage spacing
  • ✓ Smooth, barefoot safe walking surface
100% Screw-Free Surface
03
UV Defense

Trans-Oxide UV Protective Seal

Every board is pre-conditioned and treated with micronized iron-oxide marine deck oil. It filters 99% of solar radiation, preventing tropical timber from turning prematurely silver while nourishing natural wood oils.

  • ✓ Deep-penetrating botanical tung and teak oils
  • ✓ Never chips, blisters, or peels like varnish
  • ✓ Easy annual wash and buff-on maintenance
Rich Amber Grain Retention
