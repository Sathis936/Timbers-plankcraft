Engineering Protocols

Three Precision Installation Methods

The optimal installation system is dictated by subfloor construction, building acoustic bylaws, and underfloor radiant heating specifications.

01
Slab & High-Rise

Full-Spread Silane Glue-Down

Our gold-standard method for concrete slabs and high-rise condominiums. We trowel elastic silane polymer adhesive that cures into an elastomeric membrane, allowing wood to expand and contract naturally while deadening footstep sound.

  • ✓ 100% solid feel underfoot with zero hollow echoes
  • ✓ Integrated moisture barrier up to 90% slab RH
  • ✓ Recommended for Herringbone and Chevron layouts
Best for: Luxury Estates & High-Rises
02
Solid Hardwood

Blind Nail & Cleat Fastening

Designed for solid hardwoods over wood subfloors and timber joists. Using pneumatic floor nailers, serrated steel cleats are driven into the plank tongue at a precise 45-degree angle every 6 to 8 inches, locking boards immovably in place.

  • ✓ Traditional craft protocol for solid plank timbers
  • ✓ Rosin paper or silicone vapor retarder underlay
  • ✓ Enduring 50+ year mechanical connection
Best for: Traditional Timber Frame Homes
03
Acoustic Rated

Acoustic Floating Floor

Planks are precision interlocked via click or tongue-and-groove glue, resting atop a continuous high-density acoustic cork or closed-cell rubber membrane without fastening to the subfloor.

  • ✓ Highest STC/IIC impact sound decoupling
  • ✓ Safe for radiant hydronic floor heating systems
  • ✓ Fast, non-destructive installation timeline
Best for: Radiant Heat & Sound Bylaws
