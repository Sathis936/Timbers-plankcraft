Restoration Protocols

Three Precision Dustless Refinishing Systems

German HEPA planetary sanding, moisture-balanced grain conditioning, and European nano-ceramic shields preserve up to 95% of your timber wear layer.

01
Planetary Sanding

Lägler Trio Multi-Head HEPA Sanding

Using three counter-rotating abrasive discs synchronized with German HEPA negative-pressure vacuums, we strip degraded coatings and pet stains without drum-sander gouges or airborne dust.

  • ✓ 99.8% OSHA-compliant negative pressure capture
  • ✓ Eliminates chatter marks and cross-grain ridges
  • ✓ Preserves precious wood thickness for future life
Best for: Deep Scratches & Sun Discoloration
02
Grain Conditioning

Water-Popped Pore Prep & Grain Fill

We hand-trowel elastic wood dough blended from fine timber flour into all expansion joints, followed by distilled water-popping to open cellular pores for rich, streak-free stain penetration.

  • ✓ Seamless color-matched wood fiber filler
  • ✓ Opens dense annual rings for uniform color
  • ✓ Neutralizes aged water and mineral spots
Best for: Parquet Restoration & Joint Filling
03
Nano-Ceramic

Nano-Ceramic HD & Hardwax Shield

Fortified with microscopic ceramic particles, our 2-component waterborne finishes cure into an ultra-durable commercial shield that resists high-heel indentations, pet claws, and household spills.

  • ✓ Commercial-grade anti-scratch hardness rating
  • ✓ Zero-VOC & GREENGUARD Gold certified
  • ✓ 3-hour rapid dry time with walk-on capability
Best for: High-Traffic Homes & Pet Owners
