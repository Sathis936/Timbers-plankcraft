Precision Protocol

The 5-Stage Dustless Restoration Timeline

How our master sanders restore high-traffic hardwood floors without disrupting your household.

01

Laser & Moisture Inspection

Laser elevation verification, wood species moisture content checking, and deep nail countersinking.

02

Coarse Heavy Cut (36-Grit)

Eliminating deep gouges, sun damage, and previous finishes using 99.8% HEPA vacuum sealed belt sanders.

03

Full Trowel Wood Fill

Resin mixed with fine oak flour is spread across the entire floor to fill all expansion hairline joints.

04

Planetary Trio Buffing

Three rotating multi-head planetary discs eliminate all dish-out or swirl marks for glass-flat uniformity.

05

3x Ceramic Seal Application

Application of German nano-ceramic commercial sealer with zero chemical odor and fast curing time.
